## Surface
- Mode: Operate and Read.
- Job: Find an artifact by name or destination and explore its image, story, and reference.
- Constraints: Preserve the existing visual identity and direct detail URL. The only current entry and its image, story, and reference are unapproved placeholders.

## Direction contract
THESIS: A searchable collection and a generous artifact view share one workspace, making the artifact rather than the list the focus.

OWN-WORLD: The existing Nebula Archive palette, Gabarito type, starlight reading surfaces, and cyan interaction cues carry through without changing the site identity.

STORY: Visitors filter by name or Moon, Mars, and Deep Space, select an entry, and read its story and reference without leaving the collection.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A compact page introduction leads into a narrow search and category list beside a much larger selected artifact. Its image leads, with the story and reference alongside on wide screens and below on smaller screens. Selection is the signature interaction.

FORM: Existing surface refined to the requested inline selection and artifact emphasis; no concept roll for this narrowly specified refinement. The user chose “the artifact should be the highlight” and “Show its large image and details alongside the searchable list”.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
