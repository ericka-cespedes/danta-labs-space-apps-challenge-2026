# NASA Space Apps Demo Preparation

Use one format: a video of 30 seconds or less, or a slide deck of 7 slides or fewer.

## 30-Second Video Script

Read at a natural pace and keep the finished video at or under 30 seconds:

> Hi, we’re Danta Labs from Costa Rica. **Artifacts of the Past** is our board game for NASA Space Apps’ **Abandoned and Not Forgotten** challenge. We created a physical boardgame and a digital tabletop experience on our website. Players explore equipment from the Moon, Mars, and deep space and connect it to scientific discoveries and lasting legacy. The game makes space history engaging for young people, beyond simply showing what was left behind.

Suggested on-screen sequence: project title and challenge; the physical board game and its components; the digital tabletop experience on the website; a brief view of the game in use; and the team. Show the physical and digital game that the team created.

## Slide Deck Alternative

If using slides instead of video, keep the deck to 7 slides or fewer. This six-slide outline is within the limit:

1. **Artifacts of the Past** — A physical board game and digital tabletop experience by Danta Labs, Costa Rica; NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026.
2. **The Challenge** — Abandoned and Not Forgotten. Space exploration equipment has stories connected to discoveries and a lasting legacy.
3. **What We Built** — A physical tabletop board game and a digital tabletop experience available through our website.
4. **The Game’s Stories** — Players explore space equipment from the Moon, Mars, and deep space and its scientific discoveries and legacy.
5. **How It Addresses the Challenge** — The game presents equipment through its stories and scientific contributions, not only as objects left behind.
6. **The Team and Why It Matters** — Danta Labs is a six-member Costa Rican team. The game makes space exploration history engaging for young enthusiasts.

Credit images and other media on the slide where they appear. Identify sample artifact entries as examples; do not present them as verified research.
