### Detailed Project Description

**Artifacts of the Past** is a physical board game and digital tabletop experience created by Danta Labs, a six-member team from Costa Rica, for NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026’s **Abandoned and Not Forgotten** challenge.

#### What exactly does it do?

It helps players discover the stories of space exploration equipment associated with the Moon, Mars, and deep space, including the scientific discoveries and lasting legacy connected to that equipment.

#### How does it work?

Players explore the equipment and its story through the physical board game and its manual, or through the digital tabletop experience on the project website. Both formats connect the equipment with its role in exploration and its scientific and historical significance.

#### What benefits does it have?

The game turns space exploration history into an interactive tabletop activity for young space enthusiasts aged 10 and older. It encourages curiosity about science and history and presents equipment as part of a larger story, rather than only as objects left behind.

#### What do we hope to achieve?

We chose the board-game format because it is a simple, age-appropriate way to tell stories through play. For school-age learners, an interactive game can make learning more enjoyable than simply reading facts. We hope the physical and digital versions will make space exploration history accessible to young people, help them appreciate the discoveries made possible by exploration equipment, and inspire them to learn more about space science.

#### What tools, coding languages, hardware, or software did we use?

The website was built with React, TypeScript/TSX, React Router, CSS, Tailwind CSS, and Vite. The project also includes a physical tabletop board game and its components. No specialized electronic hardware is required. AI tools used during development and content creation are described in the [AI Use Disclosure](#ai-use-disclosure).
