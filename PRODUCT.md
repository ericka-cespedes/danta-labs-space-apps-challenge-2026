# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

Young space enthusiasts aged 10 and older are the intended tabletop players. NASA Space Apps judges are an audience for the project's formal presentation.

## Product Purpose

Danta Labs is developing Artifacts of the Past, a tabletop experience that connects physical play with scientific stories about space exploration equipment. It aims to connect young enthusiasts with the history, scientific discoveries, and continuing legacy of that equipment.

## Positioning

The project uses tabletop play to tell science-centered stories about exploration equipment and its discoveries and legacy, rather than presenting it only as objects left behind. The README situates the stories across the Moon, Mars, and deep space.

## Operating Context

The project is being developed by a six-member team from Costa Rica for the NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026 and is intended to be presented formally to the judges. The specific play setting and how the web app supports the tabletop experience are undecided.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The current web prototype names Moon, Mars, and deep space as exploration categories. Gameplay, the web app's role, and success criteria are still being defined. The README establishes a scientific-storytelling focus; required factual sources, language expectations, and accessibility requirements have not been specified.

## Brand Commitments

Danta Labs is the team name. “Artifacts of the Past” is the game name, and “Abandoned and Not Forgotten” is the challenge name. The project presentation should be formal for the judges.

## Evidence on Hand

The README documents the project concept and challenge context. The current prototype contains the three exploration-category labels, but no curated equipment data, research sources, or user-study evidence is documented.

## Product Principles

- Center scientific storytelling, not just equipment inventories.
- Connect equipment to its discoveries and continuing legacy.
- Keep the tabletop experience central; do not assume the web app's role before it is defined.
- Present the project clearly and formally to judges without overstating its prototype status.
